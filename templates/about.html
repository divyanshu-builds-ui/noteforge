💡 The Problem

Class notes PDFs often come with dark backgrounds, unnecessary branding (headers/footers), and one slide per page. Printing 80 slides means 80 pages — expensive, wasteful, and bulky to carry around.

🔧 The Solution

NoteForge takes your PDF, inverts the colors (dark → light for printing), trims whitespace, and stitches 4 slides into a single A4 page. 80 slides become 20 pages. Your wallet and the environment both thank you.

🚀 What's Next

Planning to add: custom grid layouts (2×3, 3×3), batch processing multiple PDFs, selective slide removal, and a mobile app. Suggestions? Reach out!
